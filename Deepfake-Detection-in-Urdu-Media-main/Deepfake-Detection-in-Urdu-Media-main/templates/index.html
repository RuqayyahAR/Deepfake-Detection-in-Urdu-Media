Welcome to the Deepfake Detection App

What is it? A simple web application that checks videos to see if they look like deepfakes (AI-manipulated media). It supports Hindi, Urdu, Telugu and English media.

What does it do? It takes a video you upload (or a website link), looks at the faces frame by frame, checks for unnatural blur, colour shifts and weird movement, and tells you if it looks authentic or manipulated.

How does it help? It helps students, journalists and everyday users spot fake videos in regional languages (Hindi, Urdu, Telugu, English) so they are not misled by manipulated media.

How to operate? Follow these three steps:

How to Use This App

1

Choose Language

Pick Hindi, Urdu, Telugu or English so the app uses the right language settings.

2

Upload Video

Upload a video file from your computer, or paste a website link to the video.

3

View Result

See whether the video is authentic or likely a deepfake, with detailed indicators and internet search results.
