⚠ What this means

  • MATCH segments: the suspect video/audio at that second is consistent with the reference (no detectable alteration).
  • VIDEO_ALTERED segments: the video content differs from the reference (possible video edit, deepfake face swap, or scene replacement).
  • AUDIO_ALTERED segments: the audio differs from the reference (possible audio dub, voice swap, or audio edit).
  • BOTH_ALTERED segments: both video and audio differ.

Source tracing is complementary to deepfake detection. A "MATCH" means the video is the same as the reference - it does not prove the reference itself is authentic. Always verify the reference video independently.
